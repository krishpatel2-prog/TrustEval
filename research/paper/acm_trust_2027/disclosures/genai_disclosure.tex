\section*{Generative AI Usage Disclosure}
\label{sec:genai_disclosure}

In accordance with ACM policy on authors' use of generative artificial intelligence and the ACM TRUST 2027 author guidelines, the authors disclose the following utilization of generative AI systems across the lifecycle of this research:

\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Exploratory Research Ideation and Initial Script Scaffolding:} OpenAI's ChatGPT models were consulted during the early exploratory phase of the project to assist with preliminary literature search query formulation, exploratory scripting for data ingestion, and draft outline structuring.
    \item \textbf{Coding Assistance and Verification Scaffolding:} Google's Gemini-powered Antigravity agentic environment was utilized as an interactive programming partner for generating automated audit and verification scripts (e.g., deterministic rater-reconciliation checks and permutation hypothesis testing scripts), assisting with Matplotlib visualization styling, and performing modular \LaTeX{} refactoring.
    \item \textbf{Visual Layout Prototyping:} Exploratory layout generation tools were queried during early design phases to evaluate multi-panel figure schematics before final Matplotlib vector rendering.
    \item \textbf{Human Authorship and Oversight:} All scientific hypotheses, research questions, experimental protocols, deterministic data pipelines, model evaluations, and statistical calculations were formulated, directed, and independently verified by the human authors. Generative AI tools served strictly as typographical, coding, and scaffolding aids. The authors have verified all citations, quantitative metrics, and analytical conclusions against frozen experimental artifacts, and take full academic responsibility for the integrity, validity, and claims presented in this manuscript.
\end{enumerate}
